To: Portfolio Committee and Delivery Leads
From: Finance Office <finance@lumenarc.example>
Date: March 8, 2027, 08:58 KST
Subject: Budget draft remains under review

Colleagues,

The latest budget draft remains under review and has not been approved by the CFO. Please treat it as a working document, not as an authorized spending limit, a final allocation, or permission to commit funds. The draft may help frame the discussion, but its circulation does not change the currently approved budget scope.

The review is balancing investment proposals against continuity of services covered by existing contracts. Those priorities may compete for the same available resources, so requests should show their assumptions and dependencies together. In particular, please identify which items are required by existing commitments, which are optional proposals, what operating services they rely on, and whether a request assumes a budget change. Keep fixed costs separate from incremental package cash so reviewers can see what is actually being requested.

At this stage Finance has no approved position on whether the draft should be adopted or which investments should proceed. A forecast or requested amount should be labeled as such and should not be represented as available authority. If a proposal would exceed the current approved scope, record the proposed total, the difference from the current baseline, the contractual obligations at risk, and the decision maker whose approval would be needed.

Please send supporting assumptions and unresolved dependencies to Finance for the review packet. We will communicate further when the review produces authorized guidance. Until then, no decision on the draft or new allocation is recorded here.

Regards,
Finance Office
