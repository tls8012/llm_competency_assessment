Meeting: LumenArc AX Portfolio Committee
Date: March 8, 2027, 08:58 KST
Record: DOC-COM-06 | Prepared by: Portfolio Committee Secretary

Discussion
The COO emphasized that contractual obligations should receive priority in portfolio discussions. The Product sponsor stated a preference for continuing the AI pilot. The CFO asked that any decisions remain within an approved budget scope and noted that the current budget draft has not been approved. Participants recognized that these priorities may be difficult to reconcile within the available allocation and that further review is needed.

The committee discussed the need to distinguish required contractual work from optional investment proposals and to make funding assumptions visible. The discussion did not establish which optional package, if any, should proceed. No additional budget authority was reported, and no participant recorded customer approval to change a contractual commitment.

Positions recorded
The COO's statement is a portfolio priority position. The Product sponsor's statement is a request to continue the AI pilot. The CFO's statement is a budget governance condition while the draft remains pending. These comments are attributed stakeholder views and should not be presented as a joint resolution.

Open matters
The approved spending scope remains the governing budget basis. The committee has not resolved how to reconcile the expressed preferences, whether the AI pilot will continue, or whether any funding change will be proposed. The responsible approvers must review the relevant commitments and financial information before a decision is recorded.

Next actions
Portfolio Office will prepare the competing priorities and dependencies for further review. Finance will provide authorized guidance when the budget review is complete. The secretary will record a decision only when one is made by the appropriate authority. These minutes capture discussion and requests; they do not constitute final approval, a scope decision, or a commitment.

Adjourned without a recorded decision.
